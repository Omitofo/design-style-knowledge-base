# Canonical Sources — Highest-Authority References

These are the books and authors repeatedly cited as foundational by practicing designers, design schools, and historians. Prefer these over secondary summaries when possible.

## Core Canon

| Book | Author | Why it matters |
|------|--------|----------------|
| *The Design of Everyday Things* | Don Norman | Affordances, signifiers, human-centered design |
| *Grid Systems in Graphic Design* | Josef Müller-Brockmann | Definitive grid methodology |
| *Thinking with Type* | Ellen Lupton | Accessible, practical typography |
| *The Elements of Typographic Style* | Robert Bringhurst | Deep, literary typography bible |
| *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information* | Edward Tufte | Data integrity and clarity |
| *Less But Better* / Rams monographs | Dieter Rams et al. | Ten Principles and product design philosophy |
| *Interaction of Color* | Josef Albers | Color relationships and perception |
| *Graphic Design Manual* | Armin Hofmann | Swiss foundation exercises |
| *Typography: A Manual of Design* | Emil Ruder | Swiss typographic thinking |
| *Don't Make Me Think* | Steve Krug | Practical web usability |
| *Atomic Design* | Brad Frost | Modern design systems methodology |

## Additional High-Value Works
- *The Form of the Book* — Jan Tschichold
- *Designing Programmes* — Karl Gerstner
- *Ways of Seeing* — John Berger (visual culture)
- *Envisioning Information* / *Visual Explanations* — Edward Tufte
- *Super Normal* — Naoto Fukasawa & Jasper Morrison (everyday excellence)

When expanding this knowledge base, ground new claims in these sources or equally rigorous primary material.
