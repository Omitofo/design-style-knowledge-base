# 04_ui-ux — Interaction, Usability & Interface Principles

This folder focuses on the interactive and experiential layer of design. Visual style must serve usable, understandable interfaces.

## Core Authority Sources
- Don Norman — *The Design of Everyday Things*
- Jakob Nielsen — Usability heuristics
- Steve Krug — *Don't Make Me Think*
- Ben Shneiderman — Direct manipulation & golden rules
- Modern platform guidelines (Apple HIG, Material Design, etc.)

- `norman-core.md` — (see also 00_principles)
- `nielsen-heuristics.md` — The 10 usability heuristics
- `krug-dont-make-me-think.md` — Practical web/usability mindset
- `design-systems-atomic.md` — Brad Frost Atomic Design and modern systems
- `accessibility-basics.md` — Inclusive design fundamentals
