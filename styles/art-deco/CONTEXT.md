# Art Deco

## Definition
Art Deco (roughly 1920s–1930s, with revivals) is a style of geometric luxury, symmetry, rich materials, and stylized modernity. It emerged from the 1925 Paris Exposition and combined machine-age geometry with luxurious craft and exotic influences.

## Core Characteristics
- Geometric patterns (zigzags, chevrons, sunbursts, stepped forms)
- Symmetry and strong vertical or horizontal emphasis
- Rich materials and finishes (or their digital equivalents): gold, lacquer, exotic woods, chrome
- Stylized figurative or ornamental motifs
- Elegant, often high-contrast typography
- Sense of glamour, speed, and modernity of the interwar period

## Dos
- Use symmetry and geometric repetition purposefully
- Balance ornament with strong overall structure
- Choose type that has period elegance or modern geometric clarity
- Signal luxury through material suggestion and craft quality

## Don’ts
- Overload with every Deco motif at once
- Apply the style to contexts that require humility or utility without adaptation
- Ignore legibility in pursuit of decorative type

## Particularities
- Art Deco sits between pure modernism and decorative tradition. It is modern but not austere.
- Excellent for brands that want heritage glamour or cinematic quality.

## How to Achieve
1. Establish a strong geometric framework (often symmetrical).
2. Introduce a limited set of Deco motifs (sunburst, fan, stepped forms).
3. Use a luxurious or high-contrast palette (black/gold, jewel tones, cream + metallics).
4. Select type with period character or clean geometric structure.
5. Apply ornament at key focal points rather than everywhere.

## When to Use
- Luxury, hospitality, fashion, entertainment, heritage brands
- Campaigns that need glamour and a sense of occasion

## When to Avoid
- Purely utilitarian or high-volume digital products
- Contexts that demand maximum neutrality or accessibility-first design

## Related
- Shares geometric interest with Bauhaus but adds luxury and ornament
- Can be combined carefully with modern minimalism for "luxury minimal + Deco accent"
